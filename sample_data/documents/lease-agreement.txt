COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made effective as of July 15, 2024, by and between:

Northwind Properties LLC, a Texas limited liability company ("Landlord")

and

Fabrikam LLC, an Oregon limited liability company ("Tenant")

1. PREMISES

Landlord leases to Tenant approximately 5,000 square feet of office space located at 200 Commerce Street, Suite 400, Austin, TX 78701.

2. TERM

The lease term begins August 1, 2024 and expires July 31, 2029 (five years).

3. RENT

Monthly base rent: $12,500.00 ($150,000.00 annually).
Payment due on the first day of each calendar month.
Late payment fee: 5% if not received within ten (10) days of due date.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT

Tenant shall deposit $25,000.00 upon execution of this Lease.

5. MAINTENANCE

Landlord responsible for structural repairs, roof, and building systems.
Tenant responsible for interior maintenance and cosmetic repairs.

6. INSURANCE

Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with minimum coverage of $1,000,000 per occurrence.

7. TERMINATION

Either party may terminate with one hundred eighty (180) days written notice. Early termination fee: three (3) months rent.

8. GOVERNING LAW

This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of Texas.
