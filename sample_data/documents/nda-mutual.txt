MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into effective January 10, 2025 by and between:

Initech Ltd., a corporation organized under the laws of England and Wales, with offices at 25 King Street, London EC2V 8AA ("Party A")

and

Contoso Inc., a New York corporation, with offices at 200 Park Avenue, New York, NY 10166 ("Party B")

1. PURPOSE

The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship regarding enterprise software integration and in connection therewith may disclose confidential information.

2. CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS

Each Party agrees to maintain all Confidential Information in strict confidence for a period of five (5) years from disclosure.

3. TERM

This Agreement is effective as of the date first written above and remains in effect for two (2) years, expiring January 9, 2027.

4. RETURN OF MATERIALS

Upon termination, each Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information.

5. NO PAYMENT

This Agreement involves no financial consideration between the Parties.

6. TERMINATION

Either Party may terminate upon thirty (30) days written notice.

7. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.
