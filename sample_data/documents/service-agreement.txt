MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 1, 2024 ("Effective Date") by and between:

Acme Corporation, a Delaware corporation with principal offices at 100 Innovation Drive, Wilmington, DE 19801 ("Client")

and

GlobalTech Solutions Inc., a California corporation with principal offices at 500 Tech Park Boulevard, San Jose, CA 95110 ("Provider")

WHEREAS, Client desires to engage Provider for technology consulting and software development services; and
WHEREAS, Provider has the expertise and resources to deliver such services;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. TERM

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for a period of three (3) years, expiring on February 28, 2027, unless earlier terminated.

2. SERVICES AND COMPENSATION

2.1 Provider shall perform services described in Statements of Work attached as exhibits.

2.2 Invoices shall be submitted quarterly in arrears. Payment is due within thirty (30) days of receipt of each invoice (Net 30).

2.3 Total aggregate value shall not exceed Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($500,000.00).

3. TERMINATION

Either party may terminate upon ninety (90) days prior written notice.

4. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
